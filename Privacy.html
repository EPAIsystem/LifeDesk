Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026  |  Last updated: August 2026  |  Published by EPAI Systems

Your privacy matters to us. EPAI Systems does not sell your personal data to third parties and does not use it for advertising purposes.

1. Who We Are

LifeDesk is a product of EPAI Systems, an online services provider offering digital products, AI consultancy and engineering services. We are based in Ghana and serve users globally.

Contact: manny@epaisystems.org

2. What Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name and email address — required to create your account
  • Location — city and country, used to localise your answers
  • Faith approach and preferences — used for personalisation
  • Questions you ask — processed to generate AI answers
  • Prayer journal entries — stored privately under your account
  • Payment information — processed by Paystack; we never store card or Mobile Money details

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage statistics — number of questions asked, verticals used, streak count
  • Device type and browser — for app optimisation
  • Question and answer history — stored in your personal library

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalised AI answers in your language and location
  • To enforce daily question limits based on your subscription plan
  • To save your history, favourites and prayer journal
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send important account and service notifications
  • To improve the quality and accuracy of LifeDesk

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore), which uses industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS encryption in transit. Firebase security rules ensure that only you can access your personal data — no other user or external party can read your information.

All communication between the app and our servers is protected by HTTPS/SSL.

5. Third-Party Services

LifeDesk uses the following trusted third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude AI — processes your questions to generate answers. Questions are sent to Anthropic's API and are subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Paystack — handles all payment processing securely. Subject to Paystack's privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase — stores your account data and question history. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • ExchangeRate-API — provides live currency conversion rates. No personal data is shared.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We only share data with the service providers listed above, strictly to deliver the LifeDesk service to you.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — update incorrect information in your profile at any time
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data from the Profile screen in the app
  • Portability — request an export of your question history
  • Withdrawal of consent — you may stop using the service at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at manny@epaisystems.org.

8. Children's Privacy

LifeDesk's Kids Desk feature is available for children aged 4–17 under parental supervision. It requires a paid plan and is only accessible through a parent's or guardian's account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children independently. Parents are responsible for supervising their children's use of the app.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data including your profile, question history, prayer journal and payment records are permanently deleted within 30 days.

10. Cookies and Tracking

LifeDesk is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We use browser local storage only to cache the app for offline use. No cross-site tracking occurs.

11. International Users

LifeDesk serves users globally. By using the app, you consent to your data being stored on Google Firebase servers. Data is protected by Google's enterprise-grade security standards regardless of your location.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above and notify users through the app. Continued use of LifeDesk after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana and the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
