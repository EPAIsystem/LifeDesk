Delete Your Account

LifeDesk — an EPAI Systems product · Last updated September 2026

You can permanently delete your LifeDesk account and all associated data at any time, directly from within the app — no email or support request required.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the LifeDesk app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Scroll to Delete My Account and tap it
  4. Type DELETE exactly as shown to confirm
  5. Your account and data are deleted immediately

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no recovery period or grace window — once confirmed, deletion happens right away.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Your profile (name, email, locality, faith approach, preferences)Yes, immediately
Your question historyYes, immediately
Your saved favouritesYes, immediately
Your prayer journal entriesYes, immediately
Your account login itself (email/password)Yes, immediately
Community questions you posted publiclyRemain visible, no longer linked to your deleted account
Payment records held by Paystack (our payment processor)Retained separately by Paystack per their own policy and applicable financial recordkeeping law

Community posts stay up because removing them would also remove other people's replies and votes on that discussion — but the post is fully disconnected from your identity once your account is deleted.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to sign in to complete the steps above — for example, if you've lost access to your account — contact us directly and we'll handle the deletion on your behalf.

Request deletion by email

Email emapanigh@gmail.com from the email address associated with your LifeDesk account, with the subject line "LifeDesk Account Deletion Request." We will confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days.
